# Devspace launch film: voice-over planning script

This is the film mapped beat by beat, for writing and timing a voice-over. All times are from the start of the final video (51.7 s, 1920×1080, 60 fps).

**How to use it**
- **Window** is when that section is on screen, and **Budget** is roughly how many words fit at a natural 150 wpm (about 2.5 words a second) with a breath.
- **On screen** is the exact text the viewer reads. The VO should either read it along, word for word, or say something different. Paraphrasing on-screen text close enough to clash with it is the thing to avoid.
- **Sync points** are moments a word should land on: a click, the music drop, a reveal.
- The VO options are starting points, not final copy. Wherever they describe the product, they only use behaviour the app actually has.

**Music:** "Cat Walk" (Mixkit), 130 BPM.
- **0–22.1 s:** quiet breakdown with no bass. This is the best room for VO.
- **22.15 s:** the drop. It's the strongest moment to have a line land on.
- **22.15–36.9 s:** full groove. Keep VO short and punchy.
- **36.9–38.8 s:** a one-bar break with no bass. A single line can sit in the gap.
- **38.8–44.3 s:** full groove.
- **44.3 s onward:** the song fades out and is silent by about 51 s, which gives the end line a clean finish.

## 1. Ambition → uncertainty (0.0 – 6.6 s) · Budget ≈ 15 words

| Time | On screen | Sync point |
|---|---|---|
| 0.1 – 1.8 | **"You want to get into tech."** (word by word; "tech." in blue) | "tech" lands about 1.0 s |
| 1.9 – 4.2 | **"But with so many paths and resources, where do you start?"** Career titles, tutorial cards and browser tabs pile in around the line | Clutter arrives about 1.9–2.6 s; "start?" lands about 3.2 s |
| 4.2 – 6.0 | **"What should you learn— and how do you put it into practice?"** Code errors, an empty project folder and a "Project ideas?" note crowd in | "practice?" about 5.6 s |
| 6.0 – 6.6 | The camera punches through the headline and the clutter clears | Whoosh peaks about 6.4 s |

**VO options**
- **A (read along):** *"You want to get into tech. But with so many paths… where do you start? What should you learn — and how do you actually build?"*
- **B (inner voice, under the text):** *"Every tutorial says something different. Every roadmap starts somewhere else."* (one line under each text card, then silence for the punch-through)
- **C (no VO):** let the text and clutter carry it, and start the voice on "Meet Devspace". This also leaves the quiet music to build tension.

> Keep the punch-through (6.0–6.6) silent or end on a breath. The cut lands harder with nothing on top of it.

## Notes for recording
- **Read-along vs. complement:** sections 1, 2 and 9 already have large on-screen text. Either read it word for word, or stay silent and let it play.
- **Leave the drop (22.15 s) and the punch-through (6.0–6.6 s) clean,** or put a single word on them.
- **The music break (36.9–38.8 s)** is a natural pause for one line, landing a payoff word as the bass returns at 38.8 s.
- The music is quietest before 22 s and after 45 s, so longer sentences sit best there.
- **Pronunciations:** *Devspace* is "DEV-space". *Devy* is "DEV-ee".
- If the VO needs more room anywhere, the film can be retimed in whole beats (0.46 s each) without breaking the music sync.
